# MouthBand — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

MouthBand ("the app") turns your voice into music. This policy explains what happens to your
data. In short: **your audio stays on your phone**, there are **no accounts**, and the only data
that leaves your device is what our advertising partner, Google AdMob, collects to show ads.

## Your audio and songs
- The app records audio from your microphone **only while you record a take** (and during the
  optional sound check and "Teach your sounds").
- All audio is **processed only on your device**. Beatbox and pitch recognition run locally.
  **Your recordings are never uploaded** to us or to anyone else.
- Your songs, takes and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are
  deleted when you delete a song or uninstall the app. They are **not** included in Android's
  cloud backup (Google Drive): the app turns it off for its own data, so nothing leaves your
  phone that way either.
- Exported files go only where you choose: a folder you pick when you tap "Save to phone", or the
  app you pick when you tap "Share".

## No accounts
The app has no sign-up, no login and no user accounts. We do not collect your name, email address,
contacts or location.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob **may collect and
process device identifiers** (such as the Android advertising ID), **ad data** (such as which ads
were shown and tapped), and diagnostic information about your device and app usage. This data is
handled by Google under its own policies:
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Consent
Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and
Switzerland), the app asks for your consent with **Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP)** before
personalized ads are shown. You can review or change your choice at any time in
**Settings → Privacy and ad consent**.

## Permissions
- **Microphone:** to record your voice while you make music. Never used in the background.
- **Internet / network state:** used only by the advertising and consent SDKs.
- The app does not request storage permissions; saving and sharing use the system dialogs.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from them.
