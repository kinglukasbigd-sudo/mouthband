No instruments? No problem. With MouthBand your mouth is the whole band.

Beatbox a beat and it becomes real drums. Hum a low line and it becomes a bass. Hum one note per bar and you get full chords. Whistle a hook and it becomes a piano, flute or synth lead. Then sing or rap on top. A friendly coach walks you through every step, and at the end you have a finished song you can save and share.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Pick a vibe. Chill, Pop, Hype, Sad or Rock sets the tempo, key and sounds. Tap the tempo or pick it yourself.
2. Make the music with your mouth. Beatbox the beat, hum the bass, hum the chords, whistle the hook. Each part is recorded over the ones before, so everything locks to the beat.
3. Sing your verse and chorus. Your own voice, cleaned up with a gentle vocal chain.
4. Arrange, mix and export. Choose which parts play in the intro, verses, choruses and outro, press Auto-mix, and export your song.

FEATURES
• Beatbox to drums: kick, snare and hi-hat, with five drum kits (Boom-bap, Trap, Lo-fi, Rock, Pop)
• Humming to bass, chords and melody, snapped to your song's key
• Teach MouthBand your own beatbox sounds for better recognition
• "Hear an example first" on every step, so you know what to do
• Review every take: compare your voice with the converted part, switch sounds, edit notes and hits, and choose how tight the timing is
• 10 instrument sounds: synth bass, deep sub, electric bass, soft keys, warm pad, organ, piano, flute, bright lead and bell
• Song arranger with intro, verses, choruses and outro
• One-tap Auto-mix, plus your own volume and mute controls
• Export as M4A or lossless WAV, save to your phone or share anywhere
• Latency calibration so your takes land right on the beat

WORKS OFFLINE
Everything happens on your phone. You don't need an account or an internet connection to make music.

YOUR RECORDINGS NEVER LEAVE YOUR PHONE
Your voice is converted on the device itself. MouthBand never uploads your recordings or songs. The microphone is used only while you record.

MouthBand is free and shows ads, never while you record or play your music.
